# 2. Five menus, the range bar and card C

1. **Menus.**
   - Split `/bdash` into the five routes in the plan.
   - Bottom menu bar on phones, top tabs on desktop.
   - A range bar under the header: 7, 30 or 90 days, or custom. The range sits
     in the link (`?range=` or `?from=&to=`) and carries across menus.
2. **Live reads.**
   - Each page queries only its own cards for the chosen range.
   - Read-only transaction, a statement timeout of about 3 s, a cache per range
     of about 10 min, and every query's duration logged.
3. **Card C** on ฟีเจอร์และ AI, one row per feature:
   - people who used it, uses and uses per person, the share of those people
     who have ever paid, and the AI cost;
   - a chart switchable between people, AI cost and paid share. Paid share is
     smoothed over 4 weeks.
   - Rule-change markers come from a short list in code. The team says what
     changed and when; each marker is added by PR.
4. **Overview.**
   - A summary sentence about what changed and what to look at; the owner
     approves the wording.
   - KPIs link to their menu.
   - A link to each other menu, with one signal each.
5. **Readability fixes** listed in the plan, on every menu.

Acceptance:

- At 390 and 1280 px, light and dark, on every menu:
  - no sideways page scroll;
  - no chart text under 11px;
  - each list shows 5 rows on phones and then "ดูทั้งหมด".
- The range carries across menus and survives a copied link.
- Card C's people counts match a direct count on the staging copy for the same
  range.
- On production data, the slowest query of each page is logged and stays under
  the timeout.
- The team opens bdash with its passkey and switches ranges and menus.
- Caddy and the bazi door still answer 404 for anything outside `/bdash/*`.
