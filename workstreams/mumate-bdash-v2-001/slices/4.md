# 4. What chat is asked about

Plan 0.4. One bazi pull request and one mootech-fe pull request.

1. **Topics card** on แชตถามอะไร, from `mate_ai_chat_meta`, up now:
   - questions and people per topic, using the 15 reading topics plus
     "นอกเรื่อง" and "คุยเล่น";
   - a line chart of the top topics;
   - the share asking about today or tomorrow against this year or next;
   - the share that was off topic or chit-chat;
   - the change against the previous range of equal length, hidden while that
     range starts before recording began on 2026-10-21, which the card says.
2. **Paying members and free members**: which topics each group asks about,
   as totals only.
3. **Consent covers analysis** (mootech-fe and bazi):
   - policy 3.1 and the consent line say the questions are also analysed to
     improve the service; the owner approves the wording;
   - the `ai_training` consent version rises;
   - a member whose latest answer is the old version is asked again, once;
   - bazi keeps a question only when the member's latest `ai_training` answer
     accepts the new version.
4. **Question card** on แชตถามอะไร, from `ai_question_log`, read-only:
   - newest first, with topic and range filters and a word search;
   - questions and people (distinct subjects) in the range, and a line saying
     these are only members who consented;
   - on phones, 5 rows and then "ดูทั้งหมด";
   - only questions recorded at or after the new wording reached production;
   - no subject, label, delete or export.

Acceptance:

- The topic counts match a direct count on the data.
- bdash adds no table and stores no question or answer text.
- The topics card says that topics are the AI's label, not the member's words.
- The question card shows no question recorded before the new wording reached
  production.
- A member who accepted the old version is asked again, and bazi keeps none of
  their questions until they accept the new one.
- The question card reaches production only with or after the new wording.
- The owner shows the business team, who keep it or ask for changes.
