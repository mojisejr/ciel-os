# 4. What chat is asked about

This slice starts when slice 1 has been live for about two to three weeks, so
the lines have something to show.

1. **Topics card** on แชตถามอะไร, from `mate_ai_chat_meta`:
   - questions and people per topic, using the 15 reading topics plus
     "นอกเรื่อง" and "คุยเล่น", with the change against the previous range of
     equal length;
   - a line chart of the top topics;
   - the share asking about today or tomorrow against this year or next;
   - the share that was off topic or chit-chat.
2. **Paying members and free members**: which topics each group asks about,
   as totals only.

Acceptance:

- The topic counts match a direct count on the data.
- No question or answer text exists in any table the slice touches.
- The card says that topics are the AI's label, not the member's words.
