# 3. Who paid, do they buy again, and the mascot in the header

On เงินเข้า, under payment outcomes:

1. **Summary for the chosen range:**
   - people who paid;
   - bought once;
   - bought again, as people and as a share;
   - average days between repeat purchases.

   A person counts as buying again when they paid in the range and have two
   or more approved payments up to the range's last day. A second payment on
   the same day as the first counts as a payment, not as buying again.

   Yearly plans show repeats only after a year, and the card says so.
2. **Payer list:**
   - columns: display name, cut short to fit a phone; times paid; total in
     baht; last payment; last package in Thai; membership end date;
   - sorted by total within the range;
   - on phones, 5 rows and then "ดูทั้งหมด".
3. **Mascot in the header** on every menu: the "mu" circle becomes the MuMate
   mascot from mootech-fe, served as a Next static file so the bdash door and
   Caddy need no change.

Acceptance:

- Payer and payment counts match `v2_payment` approved rows for the range on
  the staging copy.
- No email, phone or member id appears on the page or in its HTML.
- Long names never widen the page at 390 px.
- The mascot loads through the bdash door on staging, at 390 and 1280 px,
  light and dark.
