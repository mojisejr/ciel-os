# 1. Re-consent on a policy change

Revision 0.3. Items 1, 2 and 5 are built and on staging (fe 20bb957, bazi
9274c2a); items 3, 4, 6, 7 and 8 are changed or new.

1. **One policy file** in mootech-fe holds the version, the effective date
   and the sections. `/privacy/policy` renders it and shows the version and
   the effective date.
2. **Version.** `PDPA_POLICY_VERSION` comes from that file and rises from
   `v1`. `GET /api/user` adds the member's latest accepted `policy_version`
   from `consent`, in the same request.
3. **Gate at home and chat, and sign-up.** An onboarded member whose latest
   accepted version differs is sent to a re-consent screen:
   - heading "นโยบายความเป็นส่วนตัวฉบับปรับปรุง" and a short line, then the
     full policy as plain text, rendered from the same file as
     `/privacy/policy`; no cards, no summary, no link, no AI box;
   - one tick "ฉันอ่านและยอมรับนโยบายความเป็นส่วนตัว" and the button in a bar
     fixed to the bottom of the screen, with a read-progress line (sample A).
     The tick unlocks when the end of the policy is reached (at once if it
     fits on the screen); tapping it earlier shows a message asking the member
     to read to the end. The button needs the tick;
   - accepting appends a `consent` row with the new version, leaves
     `onboarded_at` alone, and records `ai_training` accepted at the current
     version, unless the member's latest `ai_training` answer is a no;
   - a one-time notice then says questions are used and points to ตั้งค่า ›
     จัดการความยินยอม, and the member returns to where they were going.

   Sign-up's policy step is the same screen in its first-run form: full
   policy, read to the end, one tick, no AI box; finishing records
   `ai_training` accepted, and the same notice follows.
4. **3.1 and the short AI text match the system** in the policy file,
   `aiTraining.ts` (sign-up notice and settings switch) and the bazi help
   chapter: accepting the policy turns the item on; it is switched off in
   ตั้งค่า › จัดการความยินยอม, which deletes that member's questions; only
   typed questions are kept, rewritten without names or details that point to
   a person; they are kept apart from the account under a one-way code used to
   delete them on withdrawal; they are deleted after two years. The agent
   drafts the words and the owner approves them before the merge.
5. **Pre-push warning** when the policy content changed but its version did
   not. It prints one line and never fails the push.
6. **Rewrite before keeping** (bazi `logAiQuestion`): rule clean, a model
   rewrite through the Gemini key the chat already uses that keeps the
   meaning and replaces names, dates, places and other identifying details
   with `[ชื่อ]`, `[วันที่]`, `[สถานที่]` and the like, then the rule clean
   again. A failure, an empty answer or a timeout keeps nothing. It runs
   after the reply, so the chat is not slower.
7. **Clean start.** `AI_QUESTION_SALT` gets its own value in the staging and
   production env. After the new bazi is running there, the questions created
   before it are deleted.
8. **Two-year deletion on a schedule.** A bazi cron route deletes questions
   older than two years, guarded by `CRON_SECRET` like the other jobs; a daily
   `cron.tsv` row and timer in mumate-infra call it on staging and production.

Acceptance:

- A member whose latest accepted version is `v1` sees the screen once at home
  or chat; after accepting, they do not see it again. A new member who just
  finished first-run does not see it.
- On both screens the tick cannot be set before the end of the policy is
  reached, an early tap shows the message, and the button never works without
  the tick. Neither screen has an AI box.
- After accepting or signing up, the settings switch is on, except for a
  member whose latest answer was a no; switching it off still deletes the
  stored questions.
- 3.1, the short AI text, the settings switch and the help chapter say the
  same thing, and each claim is true of the system.
- Tests show a question with a name, a slash date, a place and a phone number
  kept without them and with its meaning, and a failed rewrite keeping
  nothing.
- The deletion route deletes only rows older than two years, refuses a call
  without the secret, and the timer runs daily on both hosts.
- Staging and production have their own salt, and no question from before
  the new bazi remains.
- The policy page shows the version, and the effective date is the production
  release day.
- Tests and the build pass. The screens are checked at 390 and 1280, light and
  dark, locally with the `dev` login.
