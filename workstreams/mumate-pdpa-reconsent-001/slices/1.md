# 1. Re-consent on a policy change

Revision 0.2 changes item 3 and the acceptance below. Items 1, 2, 4 and 5 are
built and on staging (fe 20bb957, bazi 9274c2a).

1. **One policy file** in mootech-fe holds the version, the effective date
   and the sections. `/privacy/policy` renders it and shows the version and
   the effective date. The team's AI item replaces 3.1 as written.
2. **Version.** `PDPA_POLICY_VERSION` comes from that file and rises from
   `v1`. `GET /api/user` adds the member's latest accepted `policy_version`
   from `consent`, in the same request.
3. **Gate at home and chat.** An onboarded member whose latest accepted
   version differs is sent to a re-consent screen:
   - heading "นโยบายความเป็นส่วนตัวฉบับปรับปรุง" and a short line, then the
     full policy rendered from the same file as `/privacy/policy`; no cards,
     no summary, no link, no AI box;
   - one tick "ฉันอ่านและยอมรับนโยบายความเป็นส่วนตัว" and the button in a bar
     fixed to the bottom of the screen, with a read-progress line. The tick
     unlocks when the end of the policy is reached (at once if it fits on the
     screen); tapping it earlier shows a message asking the member to read to
     the end. The button needs the tick;
   - accepting appends a `consent` row with the new version, leaves
     `onboarded_at` alone, and records `ai_training` accepted at the current
     version, unless the member's latest `ai_training` answer is a no;
   - a one-time notice then says questions are used and points to ตั้งค่า ›
     จัดการความยินยอม, and the member returns to where they were going.
   Layout: the whole page scrolls with the bar fixed at the bottom (sample A,
   the agent's lean), unless the owner picks the framed box (sample B).
4. **Short AI text** stays only in `aiTraining.ts`, with a positive line. The
   bazi help chapter keeps a short summary and links to the policy.
5. **Pre-push warning** when the policy content changed but its version did
   not. It prints one line and never fails the push.

Acceptance:

- A member whose latest accepted version is `v1` sees the screen once at home
  or chat; after accepting, they do not see it again.
- A new member who just finished first-run does not see it.
- The tick cannot be set before the end of the policy is reached, and an early
  tap shows the message; the button never works without the tick.
- After accepting, the settings switch is on, except for a member whose latest
  answer was a no; switching it off still deletes the stored questions.
- 3.1 agrees with the screen: it says accepting the policy turns the item on
  and where to turn it off. The words are the team's, or the agent's draft
  approved by the owner.
- The policy page shows the version and the effective date, and the effective
  date is the production release day.
- Changing the policy text without the version prints the warning and the push
  continues.
- Tests and the build pass. The screen is checked at 390 and 1280, light and
  dark, locally with the `dev` login. Staging shows the new policy page
  after the merge.
