# 3. Iterate until the organic follower and cumulative-view targets are proved

## Result and entry

After slice 2 delivery and this slice's owner decision, operate the agreed
content-and-feedback loop until the owner can verify **more than 10,000 total
Page followers** and **more than 2,000,000 cumulative views on this workstream's
published clips**. There is no promised completion date or guaranteed virality.

## Scope

- Research references and propose Praneet's own topics; approve each story,
  produce and revise within the existing workflow, and let the owner publish.
- Review every two weeks with the owner. Derive the next experiment from the
  available evidence; changes of budget, page identity or workstream scope
  require an owner decision rather than an automatic plan rewrite.
- Establish one documented view definition/source, checked against the owner's
  Insights, before declaring the 2-million target passed. Do not combine reach,
  3-second views and plays as though they were interchangeable. Keep the latest
  comparable cumulative value for each eligible clip and avoid duplicate
  counting of snapshots, crossposts and reposts.
- Page followers are a total Page count at a dated observation, not a sum of
  per-clip follow events. Do not attribute all follower growth or sales to a
  clip without evidence. Shop link taps and orders are useful supporting data,
  not new delivery thresholds.
- Record spending and owner effort as well as creative outcomes; keep paid
  Gemini usage at most 100 THB per month. No ads, new subscription, other shops,
  Facebook integration or unattended publishing/collection is in this scope.

## Acceptance / final DoD

- Dated owner-provided Insights or another agreed verifiable source shows total
  Page followers greater than 10,000.
- The eligible-clip register with source, metric definition and latest observed
  values sums to more than 2,000,000 views, without repeated-snapshot duplication.
- The owner has a usable production and review loop in office, with enough
  source, approval, code and outcome evidence to resume in a fresh session.
- Final closeout carries the measurements, costs, material limitations and next
  owner decision. The growth target is not declared delivered solely because
  the tool works or the first clip was published.
