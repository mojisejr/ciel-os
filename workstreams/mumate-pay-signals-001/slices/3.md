# 3. Acquisition source at signup

1. **Capture** (fe): on the first visit, keep UTM parameters, the referrer
   host, the landing path and an incoming `refer_code` in a short-lived cookie
   that survives the LINE, Google and Facebook login redirect; on the
   register-login round-trip that mints the member id, write them once to an
   additive `signup_source` row for that member. Nothing is read from the
   browser afterwards.
2. **bdash card** on อยู่ต่อไหม: "มาจากไหน แล้วจ่ายไหม" — per source: sign-ups,
   still active after 7 days, paid within 35 days, share. Sources with fewer
   than five people are grouped as อื่นๆ.
3. The owner asks the team which links and codes they use today so the sources
   have Thai names; unknown sources show as ไม่ทราบ.

Acceptance:

- A sign-up through a tagged link on staging writes one row with that tag;
  a plain sign-up writes a row with no tag.
- The cookie carries nothing but the source fields and expires within a day.
- Policy 2026-10.1 covers the fields (usage and device data); no version bump.
