# 5. Expiry notice: one card before, one at expiry

The one front-end exception of this workstream, agreed by the owner on
2026-10-31 as cheaper than LINE push. Built only after slice 2 has data, so
its effect can be read.

1. **In-app card** (fe): a member whose membership ends within 7 days sees one
   dismissable card on the home screen, once; a member whose membership has
   ended sees one card, once. Wording is the team's and is service information
   ("สมาชิกของคุณจะหมดอายุวันที่ …"), not a promotion, unless the member opted
   into news. The card is driven by the server's membership state; no new
   screen.
2. **Web push** through the existing transport for members who already allowed
   notifications: the same two moments, the same wording. No LINE push.
3. **bdash**: the renewal card from slice 2 gains "เห็นการ์ดแล้วต่อไหม" so the
   notice can be read against renewals.

Acceptance:

- A free member and a member with more than 7 days left see no card.
- Each card shows at most once per member per moment.
- Dismissing the card is recorded as a signal; no other member-facing change.
- Policy 2026-10.1: the wording is checked against purpose 2 before release.
