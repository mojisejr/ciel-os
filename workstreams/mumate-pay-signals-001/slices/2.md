# 2. Daily membership state and renewals

50 active subscriptions expire within 90 days of 2026-10-31; this slice must be
on production before the first of them ends, or the renewal rate cannot be read.

1. **Daily snapshot** (bazi cron route, the `cron.tsv` pattern, one row per
   member per day, additive): tier, expiry date, paid or granted (a
   `member_subscription` row with a `v2_payment_id` is paid), legacy v1 or v2,
   active in the last 7 days or not.
2. **Renewal and lapse** derived from the snapshot and `v2_payment`: a member
   whose expiry passed and who paid again within 30 days is a renewal; one who
   did not is a lapse. Settle and refund times are added to `v2_payment` as
   nullable columns written by the webhook and reconciler from this slice on.
3. **bdash card** on อยู่ต่อไหม: "หมดอายุแล้วต่อไหม" — expiring in 30/60/90
   days split paid/granted, renewed, lapsed, share; a line saying granted Pro is
   counted apart.
4. Staging rehearsal with a test member whose expiry is moved by the owner on
   the staging copy; release with the next promote.

Acceptance:

- The snapshot runs nightly in under a second on production and never runs in
  a member's request.
- Paid and granted are never summed on the card.
- A member who renews on the staging copy shows as renewed the next day.
- No name or id on bdash.
