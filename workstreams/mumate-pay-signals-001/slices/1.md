# 1. Walls and the checkout path

Counting starts the day this ships and cannot reach back, so it goes first.

1. **Signal table** (bazi migration, additive): `pay_signal` with id, member
   id, `kind` (`wall` or `checkout`), `code`, feature or package code, tier at
   the moment, QI balance at the moment, source app, `created_at`; indexes on
   `created_at` and `(member id, created_at)`. One insert per moment, after the
   answer is sent where the route allows it, never inside the member's request
   path when it can be avoided; an insert failure is logged and never changes
   the answer the member gets.
2. **Walls recorded by the engine** (bazi): free quota exhausted for chat and
   for cards (the next use would cost QI), QI insufficient for chat, cards,
   phone reading, honeycomb and any `qi:spend` refusal (409/402), matching slot
   cap reached.
3. **Walls recorded by fe**: calendar day locked for a free member (day-detail
   and month), compatibility monthly quota reached, reminders refused to a free
   member, advanced mode fields trimmed for a free member.
4. **Checkout path recorded by fe**: `preview` called (package, whether a code
   was applied, whether the quote was accepted or refused and why), charge
   created (already a `v2_payment` row; the signal links the preview to it),
   result read. Approved and rejected outcomes stay in `v2_payment`.
5. **bdash cards**, totals only:
   - เงินเข้า: "ชนกำแพงแล้วซื้อไหม" — per wall: people who hit it, hits, people
     who bought QI or a tier within 7 days, share; sorted by share.
   - เงินเข้า: "เปิดหน้าจ่ายแล้วจ่ายไหม" — per package: people who opened
     checkout, who created a charge, who paid; with and without a code.
   - Both honour the range bar; both say the counts start on the ship date.
6. **Staging rehearsal** on the staging copy: walk each wall as a free test
   member and as a Pro test member (Pro hits nothing), open checkout for a
   tier and a QI pack, count the rows, measure response times of the touched
   routes before and after.
7. **Release** with the owner's next promote; migration run by the owner first.

Acceptance:

- Every wall in steps 2 and 3 writes one row with the member id, tier and QI
  balance; a Pro member writes none; the member's answer is unchanged in
  content and status.
- A checkout opened without a charge is visible as a person who opened and did
  not pay.
- The two cards match a direct count on the staging copy for the same range.
- Measured response time of chat, card, day-detail and preview routes on
  staging does not worsen beyond noise.
- No name, id or text on bdash; nothing new on `/ops`.
- After release, on the owner's word, a production count shows rows for at
  least one wall and one checkout within the first day.
