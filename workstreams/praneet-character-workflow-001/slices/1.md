# 1. Preserve the chosen style and extract the reusable asset workflow

## Scope

Turn the accepted Xiao Mu v04 evidence into an Office style/runbook and a
small reusable asset/build/review contract. Separate common helpers and
capture/review behavior from character-specific geometry, labels, paths,
palette and supported proportions. Do not broaden the first character family
or replace the accepted art while extracting the workflow.

## Acceptance

- Record v04's selected art direction with reference/render links and explicit
  whole-figure/front/side/back criteria. Distinguish the approved baseline from
  previous rejected trials and from untested generator capabilities.
- Document both entry paths, reference/final owner gates, agent-internal
  shape review and the exceptions that need a new owner decision.
- Define a small character brief/reference approval and delivery/cost format
  sufficient for the proof. No speculative universal rig/material library or UI.
- Reproduce the baseline with the extracted local commands, save a usable
  .blend and GLB, and capture equal-scale neutral/silhouette views and a silent
  ten-second turntable. Verify the actual output and retained earlier assets.
- Demonstrate at least a supported scalar or palette adjustment through data,
  documenting where geometry changes still require source edits. Do not
  present uniform scaling as a new character or complete proportion controls.
- Keep the production styles and old lab commands working. Make no paid or
  native image-generation request in this extraction slice.

## Evidence and limits

Record source revisions, package identities, exported coordinate convention,
actual media checks, owner-visible comparison and resource/cost measurement
limits. Shared files/commands are an interoperability design; Claude execution
remains unproved until observed. Closeout must identify the next owner decision
without reporting future reference generation as completed.
